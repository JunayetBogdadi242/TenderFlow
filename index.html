How it works

  1. Upload a valid requirements.json file.
  2. Add the tender PDFs. Duplicate content is detected automatically.
  3. Match each requirement to one PDF. Suggestions are never applied without your confirmation.
  4. Enter expiry dates where required and resolve every blocking issue.
  5. Generate the final PDF. The cover is always English and the original PDF content is preserved.
